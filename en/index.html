Eliminating the Most Expensive Construction Issues

Leaks from concealed plumbing installations are a hidden threat. Our system solves this problem at the source, regardless of the building technology.

Timber Frame Construction

In timber frame structures, the most dangerous hazard is a hidden leak inside the wall. Even a tiny seepage – unnoticed from the outside – causes moisture accumulation.

This leads to mold growth and wood rot, ultimately resulting in expensive wall demolition and structural replacement.

The Hakonor Solution: The box isolates the mixer. Any leak drop is routed through a drainage outlet outside the wall, instantly notifying you of the failure and protecting the structure from rotting.

Masonry Construction

In multi-family residential buildings, major issues arise from bathroom water leaks. Thanks to waterproof membranes and tiles, you rarely notice a leak inside your own space.

Your neighbor downstairs or behind the wall is usually the first to discover the leak, leaving you facing substantial renovation costs for damaged property.

The Hakonor Solution: The system traps water inside the box during a leak and routes it safely to a drainage outlet in your own bathroom. Avoid damage, neighbor claims, and high repair costs.

How Does It Work? Simple and Intuitive Installation

Installing the box inside wall structures is fast and well thought-out. Tailored to meet professional standards.

1

Frame Mounting

Install the box inside the wall structure. An intuitive process thanks to built-in mounting brackets.

2

Water & Drainage

Connect the water pipes and the drain pipe that will discharge water in the event of a leak.

3

Mixer Installation

Mount the concealed mixer unit inside the box. Fits most leading plumbing brands on the market.

4

Sealed Closure

Seal the front completely. Check our documentation and training programs for complete details.
